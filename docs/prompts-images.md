# Prompts for the site photos

The current photos are generated illustrations. Real photos of the MedCare team and vehicles are better and should replace them when the client sends some. Until then, these prompts keep one look across the site.

Use the same style block at the end of every prompt so the set reads as one shoot. Generate at 3:2 (1536×1024) for service cards and 4:5 (1280×1600) for the hero, export as WebP under 200 KB, then run the placeholder script described at the top of `src/lib/image-placeholders.ts`.

Keep out of every image: logos, readable text, flags, a recognisable monument of another city, more than five fingers, uniforms of a real company.

**Style block (append to each prompt)**

> Documentary photograph, natural window light, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, calm muted colors with soft blue and warm beige tones, real skin texture, candid moment, no posing toward the camera, contemporary Casablanca apartment or street, no text, no logos.

## Hero (4:5): care arriving at the door

> A Moroccan doctor in his thirties in a white coat over navy scrubs, a medical bag on his shoulder, is welcomed at the front door of a Casablanca apartment by a woman in her sixties wearing a light headscarf. She is holding the door open and smiling with relief. He is mid-step, greeting her. Seen from inside the hallway, daylight coming from the stairwell behind him.

## Ambulance and patient transport (3:2)

> Two Moroccan paramedics in plain navy uniforms load a stretcher into the back of a white ambulance parked in a residential street of Casablanca with white apartment buildings and palm trees. Early evening light. The ambulance is plain white with one red stripe, no lettering and no emblem. Focus on the teamwork, faces partly visible.

## Doctor at home (3:2)

> A Moroccan doctor sits on the edge of a sofa in a Moroccan living room and listens to the chest of an elderly man with a stethoscope. The man's adult daughter stands nearby, watching. A medical bag is open on the low table. Soft afternoon light through a curtain.

## Home nursing (3:2)

> A Moroccan nurse in light blue scrubs takes the blood pressure of a woman in her sixties seated in an armchair at home. The nurse's kit is open on a side table: gloves, dressing packs. Close framing on hands and faces, warm interior with zellige tiles in the background, slightly out of focus.

## Garde-malade (3:2)

> A Moroccan caregiver in her forties, in a plain pale tunic, helps an elderly woman drink a glass of water in a quiet bedroom in the evening. A bedside lamp is on. The caregiver's hand rests on the woman's shoulder. Calm, intimate, unposed.

## Teleconsultation (3:2)

> Over-the-shoulder view of a Moroccan woman in her thirties at her kitchen table, talking to a doctor on a laptop video call. The doctor on screen is slightly blurred. A cup of mint tea and a notebook on the table. Morning light.

## Home blood sampling (3:2)

> A Moroccan nurse wearing gloves draws blood from the arm of a man in his fifties seated at his dining table at home. Sample tubes with colored caps lie in a small tray. The man looks away calmly. Bright morning light.

## Medical transfer and repatriation (3:2)

> Two paramedics in navy uniforms push a stretcher with a patient under a blanket across an airport apron toward a small white medical aircraft with its door open. Overcast soft light. No airline markings, no text.

## Event medical cover (3:2)

> A first-aid post at an outdoor event in Casablanca: a Moroccan medic in a plain navy uniform checks a kit bag beside a folding table with medical equipment, with a blurred crowd and stage lights behind. Late afternoon.

## Care for elderly people (3:2)

> A Moroccan caregiver walks slowly beside an elderly man using a walking frame along the corridor of his home. She holds his elbow lightly. Family photos on the wall, out of focus. Warm daylight.
